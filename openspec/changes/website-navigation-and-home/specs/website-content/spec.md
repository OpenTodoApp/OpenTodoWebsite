## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Content navigation
The Resources menu of the main navigation SHALL offer Templates and Guides once at least one entry of each is published, and every content page SHALL end with the site's closing section, which offers the demo, self-hosting and import.

#### Scenario: Navigation appears with content
- **WHEN** the first template and the first guide are published
- **THEN** every page's Resources menu shows Templates and Guides, and works at 360 px without JavaScript
