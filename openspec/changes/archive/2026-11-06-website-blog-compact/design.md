# Design

## D1. Rows
Most posts are release notes or short news; a reader scans dates and titles. A row is a three-column grid (date, title and summary, category) that becomes two lines on phones (date and category, then the title and summary). The summary is clamped to two lines. Covers stay on the post page and in social cards.

## Build and download size
The index no longer loads up to twelve cover images: smaller pages, no effect on build time.
