OpenTodo is a to-do app you run on your own server. Until now, news about it lived in release notes and the odd forum thread, and was gone a week later. This blog is where it goes from now on.

## What you'll find here

<!-- no-claim -->
Posts come in four kinds:

<!-- no-claim -->
- **News:** announcements and milestones.
- **Releases:** what a new version brings and why it works the way it does. The complete list of changes is always in the [release notes](https://github.com/pixari/opentodo/releases).
- **Development:** how OpenTodo is built, including work that isn't released yet. Those posts say so clearly at the top.
- **Ownership:** essays on why your task list should live somewhere you control. The first two are already here.

## What you won't find here

<!-- no-claim -->
Promises about dates, invented numbers, or features described as available before they're in a release. If a post talks about something you can use today, it links to the documentation that shows how.

## Follow along

<!-- no-claim -->
The blog has its own [RSS feed](/blog/feed.xml). There's no newsletter and no tracking: add the feed to your reader and new posts will show up there.

<!-- no-claim -->
If you'd like to try OpenTodo first, the [demo](https://demo.open-todo.app) runs in your browser with nothing to install.
