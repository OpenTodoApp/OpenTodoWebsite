A to-do list tells you what to do; a calendar tells you when. Time blocking joins the two: you give each important task a slot, and you see at once whether the day is realistic.

## Estimate first

Add an estimate to the tasks that take real time. With [quick add](/docs/quick-add/) you type it at the end: `Draft the proposal tomorrow 9am 90m`. Rough is fine: 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes covers most things.

## Then give it a time

A task with a due time and an estimate becomes a block on the [Day and Week calendars](/docs/time-blocking/), as long as its estimate. The calendar shows planned hours per day, so an overloaded Wednesday is obvious before it happens.

## Count the meetings too

A day with three hours of meetings does not hold eight hours of tasks. Turn on **Count meetings in capacity** and the busy events of your subscribed or connected calendars count toward each day's total, so the header reads `6h 45m / 8h` and turns amber when your tasks fit but not around the meetings. [See whether the day fits](/docs/place-tasks-on-the-calendar/#see-whether-the-day-fits)

## Keep hours for one kind of work

A **frame** such as *Deep work*, weekdays 09:00 to 11:00, is a quiet band behind the Day and Week blocks. It never moves or blocks anything; it reminds you what the hours are for. Give it a project, label or saved view to plan from, and **Plan from this frame** opens that list. [Frames](/docs/frames/)

Small tasks are hard to block one by one. A **slot** such as *Admin · 16:00–16:45* is one block that holds several of them as a checklist, and counts once toward the day. [Slots](/docs/slots/)

## Plan the week, adjust the day

On Sunday or Monday morning, open the Week calendar and place your three or four most important tasks. Each morning, open the Day view and adjust: move what no longer fits, and leave gaps for the unexpected. Untimed tasks stay in [Today](/docs/views/) as a list next to your blocks.

When the morning slips anyway, the header says how many blocks are behind and offers **Re-plan**: it moves the missed blocks, in their order, into the free time left today, as a preview you apply or cancel, with one Undo. [Re-plan the rest of the day](/docs/replan-the-day/)
