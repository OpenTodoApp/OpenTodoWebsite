<!-- no-claim -->
A goal like "get fitter" cannot be done on a Tuesday. A SMART goal can: "run 5 km without stopping by 31 March, three runs a week". Specific says what, measurable says how you will know, achievable keeps it honest, relevant ties it to something you care about, and time-bound gives it an end.

## Why it works

<!-- no-claim -->
The method forces the vague wish into shapes a task list can hold: a date, a count, a repeating action. Once it is in that shape, progress is visible and the next step is never in doubt.

## Set it up in OpenTodo

1. **One project per goal**, named as the outcome with its date: "Run 5 km by 31 March". [Organise with projects](/docs/tasks-and-projects/#organise-with-projects)
2. **The measures as milestones**, dated tasks with the number in the title: "3 km without stopping (15 Feb)". Give them subtasks for the steps, and the parent shows how far you are, like 2/4. [Break a task into subtasks](/docs/tasks-and-projects/#break-a-task-into-subtasks)
3. **The repeating action** as a recurring task: "Run" every Monday, Wednesday, Saturday, with a reminder the evening before. [Make a task repeat](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#make-a-task-repeat)
4. **A view for all goals**: `search: "by " & !completed` grouped by project, or simpler, a parent project "Goals" and the view `##Goals`, pinned to the sidebar. [Save your own views](/docs/views/#save-your-own-views)
5. **A monthly check-in** task that repeats every 1st: is the goal still relevant, is the pace achievable? Adjust the date rather than quietly abandon it. [Rules you can write](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#rules-you-can-write)

## For a team

- Share the goal project with the people who contribute, assign the measures, and let the Activity feed show movement without a status meeting. [Share a project](/docs/sharing/#share-a-project)
- Comments on a milestone keep the "why we moved the date" where the next person will find it. [Comment on a task](/docs/sharing/#comment-on-a-task)

## Make it stick

- The [new job template](/templates/new-job/) is three SMART goals in a row: learn, contribute, lead, each with dated subtasks. [Tasks and projects](/docs/tasks-and-projects/)
- When a goal is reached, archive its project rather than deleting it; the record of how it went is worth keeping. [Archived projects](/docs/trash-and-history/#archived-projects)
