<!-- no-claim -->
The Pomodoro technique is a timer and a promise: for twenty-five minutes you do one thing, then you stop for five, whatever state it is in. Four of those make a set, and the set earns a longer break. It works because starting is the hard part, and twenty-five minutes is small enough to start.

## Why it works

<!-- no-claim -->
Big tasks are vague and vagueness breeds delay. A pomodoro is never vague: it is the next twenty-five minutes on this task. The breaks matter as much as the work; they keep the fourth burst as sharp as the first.

## Set it up in OpenTodo

1. **Give tasks an estimate in pomodoros.** Select a task and press <kbd>e</kbd>, then type `25` or `50`; in quick add, write `+25m`. The estimate shows on the row. [Estimate how long a task takes](/docs/time-blocking/#estimate-how-long-a-task-takes)
2. **Break anything over two pomodoros into subtasks**, each small enough for one burst. The parent shows your progress, like 2/5. [Break a task into subtasks](/docs/tasks-and-projects/#break-a-task-into-subtasks)
3. **Plan the sets on the Day calendar.** Press <kbd>s</kbd> on a task and place it; the block is as tall as the estimate, so four pomodoros and their breaks fill a visible two hours. [Place a task on the calendar](/docs/time-blocking/#place-a-task-on-the-calendar)
4. **Fill the gaps with Quick wins.** Above most lists, **Quick wins** shows only tasks of 15 minutes or less, right for the odd ten minutes before a meeting. [Narrow a list by duration](/docs/views/#narrow-a-list-by-duration)
5. **Start the timer from the task.** Choose **Pomodoro** under **Settings → General → Focus timer**: 25 minutes of work, a 5 minute break, and 15 minutes after every fourth, all four numbers yours to change. Then **Start focus** in a task's menu (<kbd>.</kbd>, the row's **⋯** or a right-click) opens Focus with the session already running. Each stretch of work is logged as time on the task; breaks are not. [The three timers](/docs/focus/#the-three-timers) [Open it](/docs/focus/#open-it)

## Make it stick

- Read the day's header before you start: if it says more than your day holds, cut a set rather than skip the breaks. [See whether the day fits](/docs/time-blocking/#see-whether-the-day-fits)
- A session ended mid-sentence? **+5 min** carries on with the same task for a few more minutes, and background noise (white, pink or brown, made on your device) plays only while you work. [A few more minutes](/docs/focus-options/#a-few-more-minutes) [Background noise](/docs/focus-options/#background-noise)
- Note interruptions as tasks in the Inbox with <kbd>q</kbd> instead of acting on them; they wait until the break. [Add a task](/docs/tasks-and-projects/#add-a-task)
- The [study plan template](/templates/study-plan/) is built from fixed blocks with estimates, a ready-made set of pomodoros on the [Week calendar](/docs/time-blocking/#open-the-calendar).
