## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- A project to share, and a way to send the link yourself (a message, an email). OpenTodo never sends it for you. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)

## Steps

1. Open **Share** on the project and select **Create a link…** under **Invite with a link**. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
2. Pick the role they get. Optionally enter their email, so only an account with that address can use the link. Choose how long it stays valid (1 hour to 30 days; 7 days by default). [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
3. Select **Create link**, then **Copy link**, and send it however you like. The link is shown only once and works once. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
4. When they open it, they see who invited them, the project and the role. **New to the server?** They select **Create an account**, choose a name and password, and land in the project. **Already have an account?** They sign in and select **Join project**. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)

## Who can bring new people

<!-- no-claim -->
A link creates a new account only when the server allows it: when the link was created by the server's owner or an admin, when open sign-up is on, or when the owner switched on **Members can invite new people with project links** in Settings → Access policies. Otherwise the Share dialog says so under the new link, and it works only for people who already have an account. [Who can bring new people](/docs/sharing/#who-can-bring-new-people)

## Good to know

- The dialog lists the links still waiting, with role, email, expiry and who created them; **Revoke** stops one. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
- To invite someone to the server without any project, the owner or an admin creates an invitation in **Settings → Administration → Members**. [Invite someone new](/docs/sharing/#invite-someone-new)

## If it does not work

- See [an invite link does not work](/how-to/invite-link-does-not-work/) for the four reasons a link stops working. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
