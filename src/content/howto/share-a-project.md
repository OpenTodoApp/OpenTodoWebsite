## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- The other person's account on the same server. If they have none yet, use [an invite link](/how-to/invite-someone-with-a-link/) instead; it creates the account and shares the project in one go. [Share a project](/docs/sharing/#share-a-project)

## Steps

1. Open the project and select **Share** at the top, or press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>. [Share a project](/docs/sharing/#share-a-project)
2. Type the person's email address. [Share a project](/docs/sharing/#share-a-project)
3. Pick a role: **viewer** (see and comment), **editor** (also add, edit, complete, delete and assign), or **admin** (also rename, archive and manage members). Select **Share**. [Choose a role](/docs/sharing/#choose-a-role)
4. The project appears on their devices on their next sync, with all its tasks, and a small people icon marks it in the sidebar. [Share a project](/docs/sharing/#share-a-project)

## Then

- Assign a task: open it and choose someone under **Assignee**, or select it and press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd>. They get a notification, and **Assigned to me** in the sidebar lists everything assigned to them. [Assign tasks](/docs/sharing/#assign-tasks)
- Comment on a task from the **Comments** tab; mention someone with `@` and they are notified. Comments work offline and are sent later. [Comment on a task](/docs/sharing/#comment-on-a-task)
- See what others changed in **Activity** in the sidebar, filtered by project, person or kind of change. [See what others changed](/docs/sharing/#see-what-others-changed)

## Good to know

- Labels and reminders stay personal, even on shared tasks. [Things to know](/docs/sharing/#things-to-know)
- An account with end-to-end encryption turned on cannot share new projects; shared projects are never encrypted. [Things to know](/docs/sharing/#things-to-know)

## If it does not work

- "No account with that email": only people with an account on your server can be added by email. Send an invite link, or ask an admin of the server to invite them. [I can't share a project with someone](/docs/troubleshooting/#i-cant-share-a-project-with-someone)
