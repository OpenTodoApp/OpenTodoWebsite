## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- A project with a handful of tasks.

## Steps

1. Open the project and select **+ Add section** at the bottom of the list. Type a name, such as `To do`, press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, and add the next: `Doing`, `Done`. [Add a section](/docs/sections-and-boards/#add-a-section)
2. Put tasks into sections: select the **+** on a section heading and type, or open a task and choose a **Section** in its panel. Tasks without a section are listed first under **No section**. [Add tasks to a section](/docs/sections-and-boards/#add-tasks-to-a-section)
3. At the top of the project choose **Board**, or press <kbd>v</kbd>. Each section becomes a column. Press <kbd>v</kbd> again to go back to the list. [Switch to a board](/docs/sections-and-boards/#switch-to-a-board)
4. Move a card by dragging it to another column (press and hold first on a touch screen), or select it and press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd>. [Move cards between columns](/docs/sections-and-boards/#move-cards-between-columns)

## Good to know

- The list-or-board choice is remembered per project on each device: your laptop can show a launch as a board while your phone shows it as a list. [Switch to a board](/docs/sections-and-boards/#switch-to-a-board)
- Deleting a section does not delete its tasks; they move to **No section**. [Rename, reorder or delete a section](/docs/sections-and-boards/#rename-reorder-or-delete-a-section)
- Sections by place (Kitchen, Bathroom, Garden) or by week (This week, Next week, Later) work as well as a flow. [Ideas for sections](/docs/sections-and-boards/#ideas-for-sections)
