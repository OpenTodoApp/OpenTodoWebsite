## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- An invitation link from whoever runs your server, or the [demo](https://demo.open-todo.app) if you only want to look.

## Steps

1. Open the link you were sent, choose a name and a password of at least 10 characters, and you are in. On the demo, select **Try the demo** instead; you get a private sandbox that is deleted after two hours without activity. [Open OpenTodo](/docs/getting-started/#open-opentodo)
2. Look at the sidebar on the left (behind the menu button on a phone): **Inbox** collects tasks that have no project yet, **Today** shows what is due today, **Upcoming** the days ahead, and your **Projects** below. [Find your way around](/docs/getting-started/#find-your-way-around)
3. Press <kbd>q</kbd>, or click **Add a task** at the top of the list, type `Call the dentist` and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The box stays open for the next one. [Add your first task](/docs/getting-started/#add-your-first-task)
4. Give the next one a date as you type it: `Buy a birthday card tomorrow at 9am`. Chips under the box show what quick add understood; if a word was picked up by mistake, select the × on its chip. [Give it a date](/docs/getting-started/#give-it-a-date)
5. Click the circle next to a task to complete it, or select it with <kbd>j</kbd> and <kbd>k</kbd> and press <kbd>c</kbd>. It moves to **Completed**, where you can reopen it. [Complete it](/docs/getting-started/#complete-it)

## If it does not work

- The link says it has expired or was already used: ask the person who sent it for a new one; links work once and for a limited time. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
- You see the sign-in form but have no account: sign-up is closed on OpenTodo servers by default. Ask the owner for an invitation. [Invite someone new](/docs/sharing/#invite-someone-new)
