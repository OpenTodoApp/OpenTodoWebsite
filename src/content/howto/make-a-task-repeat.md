## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- The task already added. Quick add does not read repeat rules.

## Steps

1. Open the task: click it, or select it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. [Edit a task](/docs/tasks-and-projects/#edit-a-task)
2. In **Repeat**, type a rule such as `every week`, `every mon, wed, fri`, `every 15th` or `every last friday`. Suggestions appear as you type. [Rules you can write](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#rules-you-can-write)
3. Check the next three dates shown under the field. If they are what you meant, you are done; the row shows a ↻ symbol. [Make a task repeat](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#make-a-task-repeat)
4. For chores that count from when you actually did them, add `after completion`: `every 2 weeks after completion`. To stop a series, add `until 2026-12-31` or `for 10 times`. [Rules you can write](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#rules-you-can-write)

## When the day comes

- Completing the task records that occurrence and moves the task to the next date. If it was overdue, it jumps to the next date from today, so missed ones do not pile up. [Complete, skip or split off one occurrence](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#complete-skip-or-split-off-one-occurrence)
- This week's run is cancelled? **Skip** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) moves on without recording a completion. This week's meeting is on Thursday instead? **This occurrence only** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>) splits it off as a normal task while the series continues. [Complete, skip or split off one occurrence](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#complete-skip-or-split-off-one-occurrence)

## If it does not work

- The rule is not accepted: rules are written in English whatever language you use for dates. Every form is listed in the [recurring task rules reference](/docs/recurrence/).
- You typed `every monday` in quick add and nothing repeats: that is expected; set **Repeat** in the task panel. [Make a task repeat](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#make-a-task-repeat)
