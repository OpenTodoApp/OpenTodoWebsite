## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- A connection to your server. [Good to know](/docs/import-export/#good-to-know)

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Data**. [Download everything](/docs/import-export/#download-everything)
2. In the **Export** row, select **Download export (JSON)**. [Download everything](/docs/import-export/#download-everything)
3. Keep the file. It is plain JSON: open it in any text editor, store it as a backup of your own, or import it into another OpenTodo server. [Download everything](/docs/import-export/#download-everything)

## What is in it

<!-- no-claim -->
All your projects, sections, tasks and subtasks, labels, reminders and saved views, including completed tasks and items in the Trash, and the shared projects you are a member of. [Download everything](/docs/import-export/#download-everything)

## Good to know

- Loading the file into another server recreates everything with its original ids, so it round-trips exactly; comments are the one thing not imported, because they belong to their authors. [Own export](/docs/import/#own-export-opentodo)
- The person who runs your server can also set up automatic backups of the whole server. [Turn on backups](/how-to/turn-on-backups/) [Backups](/docs/backups/)
