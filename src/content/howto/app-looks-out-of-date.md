## Check these, in order

1. **Close the app completely** (all tabs, or the installed window) and open it again. When the server is updated, the app fetches the new version in the background and switches on the next start, usually with one quick reload. [Updates](/docs/install-app/#updates)
2. **Has the server actually been updated?** The docs inside the app show the version at the top; compare it with [What's new](/whats-new/). If the server is on an old release, the person who runs it updates it. [Update the server](/how-to/update-the-server/)
3. **Something fails to display** and you see a message with a **Reload** button: reload. Your tasks are stored on the device and are not lost. [Something went wrong on screen](/docs/troubleshooting/#something-went-wrong-on-screen)
