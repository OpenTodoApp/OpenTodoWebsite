## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- A connection to your server; these settings are read-only offline. [Account security](/docs/account-security/)
- For passkeys, a server on an `https://` address. If **Settings** has no **Security** section, that is why; ask whoever runs your server. [Sign in with a passkey](/docs/account-security/#sign-in-with-a-passkey)

## Add a passkey

1. Open **Settings → Security** and find the **Passkeys** card. [Sign in with a passkey](/docs/account-security/#sign-in-with-a-passkey)
2. Select **Add passkey** and give it a name you will recognise, like "Work laptop". [Sign in with a passkey](/docs/account-security/#sign-in-with-a-passkey)
3. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or PIN when the device asks. Next time, choose **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in screen. [Sign in with a passkey](/docs/account-security/#sign-in-with-a-passkey)
4. Add a passkey on a second device too, so losing one is not a problem. Your password keeps working as a fallback. [Sign in with a passkey](/docs/account-security/#sign-in-with-a-passkey)

## Turn on two-factor authentication

1. Open **Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication** and select **Set up two-factor authentication**. [Turn on two-factor authentication](/docs/account-security/#turn-on-two-factor-authentication)
2. Scan the QR code with any authenticator app that supports time-based codes, or type the key shown under it, then enter the six-digit code the app shows. [Turn on two-factor authentication](/docs/account-security/#turn-on-two-factor-authentication)
3. Save the recovery codes that appear. They are shown only once; each works once in place of a code if you lose your phone. [Recovery codes](/docs/account-security/#recovery-codes)

## Good to know

- Signing in with a passkey skips the two-factor code, because the passkey already checks it is you. [Turn on two-factor authentication](/docs/account-security/#turn-on-two-factor-authentication)
- Tick **Remember this device for 30 days** on a browser you trust so it does not ask every time. [Remember this browser](/docs/account-security/#remember-this-browser)
- If you lose both your phone and your recovery codes, the owner of your server can reset your two-factor authentication. [Recovery codes](/docs/account-security/#recovery-codes)
