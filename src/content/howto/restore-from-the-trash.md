## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- Nothing. Restoring from the Trash works offline too.

## Steps

1. Open **Trash** in the sidebar, or press <kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>x</kbd>. Deleted tasks and projects are listed newest first, with how many days they have left. [Restore something from the Trash](/docs/trash-and-history/#restore-something-from-the-trash)
2. Select **Restore** next to the item (with the keyboard: <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> to select, <kbd>r</kbd> to restore). A task returns to its project; a project returns to its place with all its tasks. [Restore something from the Trash](/docs/trash-and-history/#restore-something-from-the-trash)

## An edit rather than a deletion?

1. Open the task and select the **History** tab. Changes are grouped into versions, newest first, with who made each and from where. [See what changed on a task](/docs/trash-and-history/#see-what-changed-on-a-task)
2. Next to a change, select **Restore this value**; or **Restore this version** to put back everything that version changed. Restoring is itself a change, so you can undo the undo. [Bring back an earlier value](/docs/trash-and-history/#bring-back-an-earlier-value)

## Good to know

- Items are deleted for good after 30 days in the Trash, or when you choose **Delete permanently** or **Empty Trash**. Your server's owner can change that period. [Delete for good](/docs/trash-and-history/#delete-for-good)
- An archived project is not in the Trash: bring it back from **Settings → Projects → Archived projects**. [Bring back an archived project](/docs/trash-and-history/#bring-back-an-archived-project)
- History is loaded from your server, so it needs a connection. [See what changed on a task](/docs/trash-and-history/#see-what-changed-on-a-task)
