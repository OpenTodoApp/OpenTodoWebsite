## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- Five minutes in the morning.

## Steps

1. Open **Today** (<kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>t</kbd>). Overdue tasks sit in their own red group at the top. For each one: do it now, give it a new date, or drop it. [Plan your day with Today](/docs/views/#plan-your-day-with-today)
2. Give the tasks that matter an estimate: select one, press <kbd>e</kbd>, type `20`, `45m` or `1h30`. The estimate shows on the row. [Estimate how long a task takes](/docs/time-blocking/#estimate-how-long-a-task-takes)
3. Select a task and press <kbd>s</kbd>: the Day calendar opens with the task ready to place. Move it with the arrow keys and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Or drag tasks from the **Unscheduled** lane onto a time slot with the mouse. [Place a task on the calendar](/docs/time-blocking/#place-a-task-on-the-calendar)
4. Read the day's header: it adds up your estimates against your day length, for example `4h 15m / 8h`, and marks the day when you have planned more than it holds. Move something if it does. [See whether the day fits](/docs/time-blocking/#see-whether-the-day-fits)
5. Short gap between meetings? Turn on **Quick wins** above a list to see only tasks of 15 minutes or less. [Narrow a list by duration](/docs/views/#narrow-a-list-by-duration)

## Good to know

- Times you set on the calendar are ordinary due dates, so they show in Today and Upcoming as well, and in calendar apps connected over CalDAV. [Tips](/docs/time-blocking/#tips)
- The grid's start, end and your day length are set per device in **Settings → Preferences → Calendar**. [Adjust the grid](/docs/time-blocking/#adjust-the-grid)
- For the week, open **Upcoming** or **Week** (<kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>w</kbd>). [Plan your week with Upcoming](/docs/views/#plan-your-week-with-upcoming)
