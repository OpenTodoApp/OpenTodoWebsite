## What you need

<!-- no-claim -->
- A task with a due date, if you want a reminder relative to it. A reminder at a fixed moment works on any task.

## Steps

1. Open the task and select **+ Add reminder** under **Reminders**. [Add a reminder](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#add-a-reminder)
2. Pick **At due time**, **10 minutes before**, **30 minutes before**, **1 hour before** or **1 day before**; or **Custom offset…** for any number of minutes, hours or days; or **At a specific date and time…** for a fixed moment. [Add a reminder](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#add-a-reminder)
3. Add more if you like: a task can have several reminders. They move with the due date, and on a repeating task they carry over to the next occurrence. [Add a reminder](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#add-a-reminder)

## When it goes off

- While OpenTodo is open, a reminder pops up in the corner with **Snooze 10 min**, **1 hour**, **Tomorrow 09:00** and **Dismiss**. No permission or connection needed. [When a reminder goes off](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#when-a-reminder-goes-off)
- To get it while the app is closed, turn on notifications for each device in **Settings → Notifications**. Snoozing on one device applies to all of them. [Get reminders when the app is closed](/docs/notifications/#get-reminders-when-the-app-is-closed)

## Good to know

- A task with a date but no time counts as 09:00 for "before" and "after". Give it a time if you need the reminder earlier. [Tips](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#tips)
- Reminders are personal: on a shared task, yours are yours alone. [Shared projects](/docs/recurring-and-reminders/#shared-projects)

## If it does not work

- Nothing arrives on your phone while the app is closed: follow [reminders on your phone](/how-to/reminders-on-your-phone/), which walks through the delivery check. [Notifications](/docs/notifications/#check-that-it-works)
