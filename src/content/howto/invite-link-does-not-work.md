## Check these, in order

1. **Has it been used or revoked, or has it expired?** A link works once, stops at its expiry (1 hour to 30 days), and when whoever created it revokes it or is no longer an admin of the project. Ask for a new one; the Share dialog lists the links still waiting. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
2. **Does the person already have an account?** Then the link's page asks them to **Sign in to join**, not to create an account. If they are already a member of the project, the page says so. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
3. **Was the link tied to an email address?** Only an account with that address can use it. [Invite with a link](/docs/sharing/#invite-with-a-link)
4. **Is the link allowed to create an account?** A project link creates new accounts only when it was made by the server's owner or an admin, when open sign-up is on, or when the owner switched on **Members can invite new people with project links**. Otherwise the page tells the person to get an account first. [Who can bring new people](/docs/sharing/#who-can-bring-new-people)

## The fix

- If you are a member and your links cannot create accounts, ask whoever runs the server to invite the person from **Settings → Administration → Members**, or to switch on the option above. Once they have joined, share the project with them by email. [Invite someone new](/docs/sharing/#invite-someone-new)
