## Check these, in order

1. **Check the email address and that Caps Lock is off.** Passwords are at least 10 characters. [I can't sign in](/docs/troubleshooting/#i-cant-sign-in)
2. **Wait a little after several failed attempts.** The server makes you wait before you can try again. [I can't sign in](/docs/troubleshooting/#i-cant-sign-in)
3. **Forgot your password?** OpenTodo does not send reset emails. Ask the owner or an admin of your server for a reset link; it works once. [Change your password](/docs/account-security/#change-your-password)
4. **Lost your two-factor phone?** Use one of your recovery codes in place of the code. Without them, the server owner can reset your two-factor authentication. [Recovery codes](/docs/account-security/#recovery-codes)
5. **You are the owner and locked out?** The reset link comes from a command on the server itself. [Recover the owner password](/how-to/recover-the-owner-password/)

## Good to know

- With a passkey on a second device, a forgotten password is not a problem: choose **Sign in with a passkey**. [Sign in with a passkey](/docs/account-security/#sign-in-with-a-passkey)
- The demo has no sign-in at all: **Try the demo** creates a private sandbox. If it says the sandbox expired, start a new one. [The demo says my sandbox expired](/docs/troubleshooting/#the-demo-says-my-sandbox-expired)
